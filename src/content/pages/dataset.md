<h2 class="has-text-align-center has-large-font-size wp-block-heading"><strong>Dataset</strong></h2>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Our data is publicly available on the <a href="https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/CKNNVT">Harvard Dataverse</a> (.tsv and .dta files), and available for download below (.csv file). The dataset contains variables for the 58 countries in our sample, including:</p>



<ul class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-list"><li>The overall de jure score</li><li>The overall de facto score</li><li>Whether the country is a signatory to the 1951 Refugee Convention, the 1967 Protocol, and the ICESCR</li><li>Access to and enforcement of work permits</li><li>Access to and enforcement of business permits</li><li>Freedom to travel domestically and choose residence</li><li>Availability of recourse for workplace violations</li><li>Access to primary, secondary, and tertiary education</li><li>Access to healthcare, official documents, formal financial services, and credential validation</li></ul>
