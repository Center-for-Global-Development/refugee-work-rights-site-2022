<h2 class="has-text-align-center has-large-font-size wp-block-heading"><strong>Refugee Work Rights Initiative</strong></h2>



<div class="wp-block-group is-vertical is-layout-flex wp-container-core-group-is-layout-4fc3f8e1 wp-block-group-is-layout-flex">
<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">The Refugee Work Rights Initiative is currently led by the Center for Global Development (CGD), Refugees International, and Asylum Access. The Initiative was <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://asylumaccess.org/what-we-do/policy-change/#:~:text=The%20Refugee%20Work%20Rights%20Campaign,chance%20at%20a%20new%20life." target="_blank">launched</a> by Asylum Access in 2013 to build a groundswell of support for the idea that refugees should have access to opportunity, entrepreneurship, and a fair chance at life.&nbsp; The 2014 “<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://asylumaccess.org/global-refugee-work-rights-report/" target="_blank">Refugee Work Rights Report</a>,” was a groundbreaking piece of analysis looking at the rights refugees have in law in many hosting countries around the world.</p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">CGD and Refugees International currently lead the <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.cgdev.org/project/expanding-labor-market-access-refugees-and-forced-migrants" target="_blank">“Let Them Work” initiative</a>, a project to generate evidence on the impacts of expanding economic inclusion for refugees and forced migrants around the world, and develop policy recommendations for global and national actors.</p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Our organizations came together in 2022 to expand the “<a href="/learn-about-refugee-work-rights/key-research/" data-type="URL" data-id="/learn-about-refugee-work-rights/key-research/">Refugee Work Rights Report</a>,” increasing the global coverage and including both the rights refugees have in law and the access they have in practice. To learn more about how we did this, please visit our <a href="/scorecard-methodology/">Scorecard Methodology page</a>.</p>
