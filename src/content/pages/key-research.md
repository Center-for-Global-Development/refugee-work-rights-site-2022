<h2 class="has-large-font-size wp-block-heading"><strong>Read the Report</strong></h2>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">The 2022 Global Refugee Work Rights Report provides the latest research on labor market conditions for people who have been displaced to 51 countries around the world. Building off of a <a href="/dataset/" data-type="page" data-id="606">unique dataset</a>, the report explores de jure and de facto conditions that refugees face, along with policy recommendations for implementing organizations and policymakers to consider in the coming years. </p>

<h2 class="has-large-font-size wp-block-heading"><strong>Other Related Research and Initiatives</strong></h2>
